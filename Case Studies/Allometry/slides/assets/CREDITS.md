# Sources of the images on the introductory slides

| File | Shows | Author | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cocoa-trees.jpg` | Cocoa trees with red and yellow pods in a plantation in Côte d'Ivoire (8 October 2014; Wiki Loves Africa 2014) | Hanay | CC BY-SA 4.0 | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cacao_fruit_in_C%C3%B4te_d%27Ivoire_(15).JPG |
| `sun-and-shade.png` | A: full-sun cocoa field in Côte d'Ivoire; B: heavily shaded cocoa field in Ghana | Goudsmit et al. | Unpublished; see below | Supplementary information 4 of the manuscript in `../../Supplemental manuscript allometry.docx` |

`cocoa-trees.jpg` was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on 25 November 2026 as the
960-pixel preview, cropped to a square (rows 180 to 1140 of 1280) and resized to
800 × 800 pixels. Under CC BY-SA 4.0, the cropped version is shared under the same
licence; the slide carries a short credit line.

`sun-and-shade.png` is the figure of Supplementary information 4 of the manuscript by
Goudsmit et al. on cocoa allometry (unpublished), extracted unchanged from the Word file
(520 × 253 pixels). The photos belong to the authors of the manuscript, of whom the course
author is one. **Permission to show them to students should be confirmed by the author**
before the slides are published; the slide credits them as "Photos: Goudsmit et al.
(unpublished manuscript)". Photo B is from Ghana, which is not a site of the data; the
manuscript does not say where in Côte d'Ivoire photo A was taken.

`fields.png` and `agb-diameter.png` are drawn by `figures.R` from `../../allometry.csv`.
